## 2. Facts by question part

### A. Notification never published (pay-api side)

**What gets published**
- `PaymentTransaction.update_transaction`: no receipt details → transaction `FAILED`. Otherwise payment `COMPLETED` and invoice `PAID`.
- If `paid_amount < invoice_amount`, the notes record a critical log plus Sentry, and the payment is not completed.
- `publish_status` publishes `PAYMENT` (payload from `create_event_payload`) to `get_topic_for_corp_type(corp_type)`.
  - Transaction `COMPLETED` and invoice `PAID` → `COMPLETED`.
  - Transaction `COMPLETED` but invoice not `PAID` → logs and returns, nothing published.
  - Transaction not `COMPLETED` → `TRANSACTION_FAILED`.
- Topic mapping:
  - `NRO` → `NAMEX_PAY_TOPIC`
  - product `BUSINESS` / `BUSINESS_SEARCH` → `BUSINESS_PAY_TOPIC`
  - `STRR` → `STRR_PAY_TOPIC`
  - anything else → None
- `publish_to_queue` skips with a log when the topic is None.
- `_release_payment` never publishes for `CSO, RPT, PPR, VS`.

**Failure handling**
- `publish_status` catches every exception and sets the transaction `EVENT_FAILED` without re-raising. The invoice and payment stay `PAID` / `COMPLETED`.
- `_release_payment` (used by BCOL, EJV, internal and sandbox paths via `complete_post_invoice`) catches the failure, logs it, calls Sentry `capture_message(level='error')`, swallows it and returns None. The notes show no `EVENT_FAILED` marker there.
- `gcp_queue_publisher.publish_to_queue` itself has no retry or error handling.
- The only retry that is visible is in `update_transaction`. If the payment is already `COMPLETED` and the transaction is `EVENT_FAILED`, it sets the transaction `COMPLETED` and publishes again. Anything else is `COMPLETED_PAYMENT`. This runs only if something calls `update_transaction` again for that transaction. The notes do not say what does.
- The flow brief says nothing repairs a lost lock/unlock event, and lists only these repairs:
  - `ERRORED` re-selection
  - the 10 s GET-after-create recovery
  - `handle_unlinked_refund_requested_invoices`
  - `UPDATE_STALE_PAYMENTS`
- `UPDATE_STALE_PAYMENTS` covers stale CC/DIRECT_PAY transactions after 30 min, and created direct-pay invoices after 2 days.

  - PAD and EFT therefore do not publish through this path. Their completion arrives via payment-jobs and pay-queue reconciliation, and the notes do not show that publishing.
  - Verify who publishes `PAYMENT`/`COMPLETED` for PAD, EFT, ONLINE_BANKING, DRAWDOWN (BCOL), EJV and INTERNAL.

- Pub/Sub's own redelivery depends on the consumer returning non-200. See C.

### C. Consumer side (business-pay)

**Entry and auth**
- `POST /worker`, `verify_gcp_jwt`. It needs `email_verified` and `claim["email"] == SUB_SERVICE_ACCOUNT`, with audience `SUB_AUDIENCE`. Otherwise 403.

**Dropped with 200 (not retried) when any of these holds**
- `not request.data`
- the event is not a `SimpleCloudEvent`
- `status_code != "COMPLETED"`, which includes `TRANSACTION_FAILED`
- `corp_type_code in ["MHR","BTR"]`
- filing not found AND `filing_identifier == None` AND `corp_type_code == "BC"`
- `payment_completion_date` already set. This is the only idempotency guard.

**Not found otherwise**
- 404, so the message is retried.
- `Filing.get_filing_by_payment_token` (matches `f.payment_id = :pay_token`) catches `Exception`, prints it and returns None. A DB error therefore looks like "not found" (404, or the BC drop).

**On success**
- It sets `payment_completion_date`, `payment_status_code="COMPLETED"` and `status=PAID`, then `filing.save()` commits.
- This happens before any publish, with no transaction or rollback around the later steps.
- `continuationIn` → `create_staff_review`, which sets Review and Filing `AWAITING_REVIEW` and does not publish to the filer.
- Otherwise `publish_to_filer` publishes `{filing:{id}}` only if `effective_date <= payment_completion_date`.
- `publish_to_emailer` always runs and publishes `{email:{filingId,type,option:"PAID"}}`.
- The branch step and the emailer publish are each in `with suppress(Exception)`. Nothing is re-raised, retried or sent to Sentry, and the message is still acked with 200.

**Delivered twice**
- The second delivery finds `payment_completion_date` set and returns 200 with no republish.
- A duplicate can still occur if `save()` succeeded but the first delivery was not acked. In that case the filer publish may have been skipped or swallowed, and the redelivery is dropped.
- There is no duplicate guard on the Queue extension.
- The entity-pay variant has `COMPLETED` status already → warn and Sentry, return.

**Resulting state when the filer or emailer publish is lost**
- The filing stays `PAID` with `payment_completion_date` set, unfiled.
- Confirmation emails are lost, and the filer and emailer are not applied or sent.
- Staff never see an error.
- No job repairs a lost immediate filer or emailer publish.
- The future-effective job re-offers only future-effective filings. It calls `GET /internal/filings/future_effective` (needs `UserRoles.system`) and publishes with no duplicate guard.
- Verify that endpoint's query. It is not shown whether it would pick up a PAID immediate filing.

**Future-effective filings**
- They are intentionally not published at payment time and are left to the future-effective job.

### D. Processing dies partway (filer and emailer)

**entity-filer**
- It creates a versioning transaction and runs the processors.
- It then sets `transaction_id`, `set_processed()` and `_meta_data`, and commits.
- Post-commit steps follow: affiliation, `consume_nr`, `update_business_profile`, `update_entity`, `publish_mras_email`, `publish_email_message`, `publish_event` and the GCP event.
- Post-commit failures are swallowed with Sentry and the filing stays `COMPLETED`.
- A processor failure before the commit means no commit. `OperationalError` and `FilingException` are re-raised, so the message is redelivered. `QueueException` and other exceptions are Sentry'd and swallowed, and the message is dropped. The filing stays `PAID`, uncompleted.
- `COMPLETED` status on redelivery → warn and return, which gives idempotency.
- `replace_file_with_certified_copy` (Minio) has no error handling. The exception reaches the handler's catch-all, the DB is not committed and the message is dropped.

**entity-emailer**
- Tracker order: `start_tracking_message` → `process_email` → `send_email` → `complete_tracking_message`.
- `send_email` POSTs to `NOTIFY_API_URL`. Non-200 → `EmailException`, re-raised (redelivered) and the tracker is marked FAILED.
- `OperationalError` is re-raised. `QueueException` and other exceptions are marked failed and dropped.
- Retries are bounded by `message_seen_count > MSG_RETRY_NUM`.
- Receipt or PDF fetch failures are logged only, and the email is sent without the attachment.
- Auth lookup failures return None recipients silently, and a processor returns `{}`.
- There is no handling shown for an existing PROCESSING or COMPLETED tracker record.

### E. What the customer-facing UI offers
- `TodoList.checkIfProcessed` polls `fetchFiling` up to 5 times at 1 s intervals, looking for PAID, COMPLETED or AWAITING_REVIEW. After that it only logs.
- `TodoList` offers Resume Payment and Cancel Payment (PATCH, which resets the filing to DRAFT only on a 200/202 payment delete). No job repairs an abandoned payment.
