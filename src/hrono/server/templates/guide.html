Words

topic
A group of related sections, or of other topics, plus a brief. The node of the tree.
section
About 10 KB of one file. What gets grouped, and what an agent reads when it explores.
condensation
What the first pass wrote about one section — identifiers verbatim, plus GOTCHA: lines. Cached by content, so an unchanged file is never condensed twice.
brief
A topic's summary, written from its condensations rather than from raw text. Reference lists are appended by code, never by the model.
passage
About 800 B. What recall injects. A section reassembles exactly from its passages.
tree
Topics all the way up to one root, over a named set of sources. A database can hold several.
stale
A brief that no longer matches what its topic holds — the members changed, or the prompt, or the model.
pin
A hand edit the pipeline must not undo. Pinning is what makes a correction survive the next run.
parked
A section ingested and embedded but in no topic yet. On the waiting list, not lost.
misfit
A section whose own topic's centre is not the nearest one.

Not used here: cluster is the algorithm's verb — what it makes is a topic. Chunk was an earlier name for a section.
